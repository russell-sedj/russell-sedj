## About

I build web platforms end to end, from requirements analysis and data modelling to frontend, backend, deployment and technical documentation.

I've delivered products in **healthcare, public administration, e-commerce and e-sports**, both within teams and as the sole developer responsible for design, build and production. What I enjoy most: simple tools that solve real business needs and stay maintainable.

My practice covers the full stack: **Angular, React and Vue/Nuxt** on the front; **Java/Spring Boot, C#/ASP.NET Core, PHP/Laravel and Node/Express** on the back, with **Docker, CI/CD and Linux** as part of how I ship. I'm now deepening my **cloud, DevOps and software architecture** skills to grow into infrastructure-aware engineering.
